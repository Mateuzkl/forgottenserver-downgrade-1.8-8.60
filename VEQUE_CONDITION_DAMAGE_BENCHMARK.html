Important qualifications

TSan compiled, but WSL2 rejected its shadow-memory mapping before main; that result is inconclusive. No Windows compiler was installed. Cache figures are Cachegrind simulations, not hardware perf counters. ConditionDamage grows from 96 to 104 bytes, while measured allocation calls, peak heap, and peak RSS fall.

Full methodology, commands, medians, min/max/p95, sanitizer output, and limitations are documented in VEQUE_CONDITION_DAMAGE_BENCHMARK.md.
